THE PROBLEM: Urban commuters wait 15–20 minutes at packed stops while buses run empty two streets away.
THE GAP: Not shortage of total buses – but lack of real-time, demand-responsive fleet rebalancing.
🚨
Choked Corridors
Sathy Road & IT corridor (Route 12) hit 134% peak occupancy, leaving passengers stranded on platforms.
📉
Idle Fleet Waste
Trichy Road (Route 3A) runs simultaneously with 60% empty seats, burning unneeded depot diesel and driver hours.
⚠️
Breakdown Domino
When a bus stalls at Ganapathy, commuters have zero visibility; next units arrive packed with no relief broadcast.
📋
Static Timetables
Fixed schedules cannot adapt to morning office spikes, Peelamedu waterlogging, or CODISSIA fair surges.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

TransitIQ: Dynamic Fleet Reallocation — Shift idle buses from low-demand corridors to overloaded peak corridors in real time.
📡
1. Live Sensing
Flags routes in real time: Red (>100% full), Yellow (Seats Free), and Green (Normal).
⚡
2. Fleet Balancing
Zero new buses needed: cancels 1 surplus trip on Route 3A and injects 2 extra relief buses into Route 12.
🗣️
3. Bilingual AI
Conversational assistance in authentic Tamil & English voice/text for less-crowded bus options and fares.
🗺️
4. Hazard Telemetry
Automatic detour notices for monsoon waterlogging near Peelamedu and tracked relief buses with live ETA.
