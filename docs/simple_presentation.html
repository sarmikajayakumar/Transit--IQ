PROBLEM STATEMENT 02 / 07

The Commuter Dilemma: Overcrowded vs. Idle

Static timetables fail to respond to dynamic morning and evening passenger spikes.

🔴 1. Overcrowded Corridor (Route 12)
Gandhipuram ↔ Saravanampatti IT Park:
  • Hits 134% peak capacity (480 passengers/hr).
  • Commuter wait times climb to 15.4 minutes.
  • Severe passenger overflow at Ganapathy & Prozone stops.
🟡 2. Undercrowded Corridor (Route 3A)
Ukkadam ↔ Singanallur via Trichy Road:
  • Operates at only 40% occupancy (60% empty seats).
  • Depots burn unnecessary diesel on underutilized runs.
  • Fixed frequency ignores the real passenger surge.
THE CORE GAP: Cities don't suffer from a lack of total buses — they suffer from inflexible, static fleet distribution.

PROPOSED SOLUTION 03 / 07

TransitIQ: Dynamic Fleet Rebalancing

Using real-time telemetry to shift buses where people are waiting, with zero new vehicle costs.

1. Add Buses to Peak Corridors
Automatically deploys +2 extra relief buses to overcrowded Route 12 (reducing headway from 15 min to 7.5 min).
2. Scale Down Idle Routes
Cancels 1 surplus empty departure on Route 3A. Preserves a reliable 15-minute headway while eliminating fuel burn.
3. Prior Incident Notification
Instant alerts for broken-down buses (e.g. Ganapathy Signal) with tracked standby relief unit (ETA 4 min) and alternate route options.
✅ Zero CapEx: Balances existing buses instead of demanding capital expenditure.
✅ Bilingual Ground Access: Pure Tamil & English assistance for all citizen groups.

HOW IT WORKS 04 / 07

The 3-Stage Rebalancing Pipeline

Continuous closed-loop optimization between passengers, roads, and depot controllers.

STAGE 1: SENSE
• Ingests stop demand & bus occupancy.
• Evaluates corridor congestion thresholds.
• Flags routes: Red (>100%), Yellow (<50%), Green (Normal).
STAGE 2: BALANCE
• Optimization Engine generates dispatch directive.
• Reallocates surplus bus capacity from Route 3A to Route 12.
• Headway drops from 15 mins to 7.5 mins.
STAGE 3: BROADCAST
• Commuters receive live route recommendations.
• AI Assistant speaks and texts in Tamil/English.
• Detour guidance around Peelamedu waterlogging.

WEBSITE FEATURES 07 / 07

Main Features of the TransitIQ Web App

A brief overview of the live operational interface accessible to commuters and depot managers.

🎨 Color-Coded Crowd Indicators
Instant visual status: Red for overcrowded, Yellow for undercrowded (seats available), and Green for normal routes.

Interactive Google Maps canvas overlaying live bus locations, congestion hotspots, and monsoon waterlogging detours.

Sub-second conversational copilot in authentic Tamil & English with natural voice synthesis and route recommendations.

One-click controls for depot operators to test peak morning rush hour surges, breakdown alerts, and fleet optimization live.
