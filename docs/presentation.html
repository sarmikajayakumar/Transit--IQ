THE COMMUTER REALITY 02 / 08

The Twin Headache in Coimbatore Transit

🔴 Problem 1: Overcrowded Sathy Road (Rte 12)
During morning hours, buses from Gandhipuram to Saravanampatti IT Park run at 134% occupancy.
  • Passengers stranded at Ganapathy & Prozone.
  • Average wait times climb past 15.4 minutes.
  • Single bus breakdown leaves 48+ people stranded.
🟡 Problem 2: Idle Seats on Trichy Road (Rte 3A)
At the exact same time, buses running between Ukkadam and Singanallur are at only 40% capacity.
  • 60% of seats run empty with high frequency.
  • Depots burn unnecessary fuel and driver hours.
  • Static schedules can't adapt to real-time morning demand.

HOW TRANSITIQ WORKS 03 / 08

Three Steps: Sense, Balance, Notify

1. Sense Corridor Crowds
Monitors route capacity in real time. Red flags corridors operating above 100% capacity and highlights underutilized corridors below 50%.
2. Tactical Fleet Balancing
Instead of buying new buses, reallocate existing fleet capacity: cancel 1 idle scheduled departure on Route 3A and inject 2 extra buses into Route 12.
3. Bilingual Broadcast
Commuters receive immediate guidance in Tamil and English: which bus has guaranteed seats, and when standby relief units will arrive.
